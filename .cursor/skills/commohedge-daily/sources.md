# CommoHedge source rules

## Tier 1 (preferred)

Exchanges and official bodies:

- ICE, CME / CBOT / COMEX, LME, Bursa Malaysia
- GIE AGSI+, ENTSOG
- EIA, IEA, OPEC, USDA
- Central banks, government statements
- Company filings and official press releases

## Tier 2

Wire agencies and major financial / specialist press:

- Reuters, Bloomberg, AP, AFP, WSJ, FT, CNBC, MarketWatch / Dow Jones
- Argus, S&P Global Platts, Kitco, mining.com

## Tier 3 (cross-check only)

Label every use as **indicative**:

- Trading Economics, Investing.com

## Never use

- Social media, forums, blogs, unknown newsletters
- AI-generated or SEO "price today" pages
- Crypto / promotional sites
- Pages with live ticker widgets (often stale) or **no visible date**

## Freshness and conflicts

- Check publication date/time on every page. Prices **>24h old** are stale: say so or drop.
- Prefer **two independent sources** per figure when possible. If they differ, record both, explain (contract, timestamp, spot vs futures, settlement vs intraday), use exchange or wire figure.
- Claims by armed groups, governments, or a single outlet stay **unconfirmed** unless two Tier 1/2 sources confirm.
- Verify "highest since", "record", "first time" against history. Verify storage targets, tariffs, quotas in **official** sources — else do not state them.
